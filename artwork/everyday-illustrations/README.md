# Everyday whiteboard illustrations

This pack contains 100 individual transparent PNGs, ready to add to Whiteboard Studio or other editors. `manifest.json` lists each drawing, its category, and its file. The `sheets` folder holds the eight source sheets used for the 80 illustrated subjects.

## Contents

- 20 men and women showing ten emotions each
- 10 hand gestures
- 10 work and study objects
- 10 communication objects
- 10 home and daily life objects
- 10 wellness objects
- 10 nature and weather objects
- 10 arrows
- Numbers 0–9

The eight illustration sheets were made with built-in image generation using alpha-transparent backgrounds and a consistent rounded, colored whiteboard style. The arrows and numbers were drawn directly in Whiteboard Studio for exact shapes and digits. Every item was isolated and exported as its own PNG. The 100-file pack was checked for transparent backgrounds and visible margins around the artwork.
