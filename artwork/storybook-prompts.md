# Storybook illustration prompts

Use one prompt per sheet in ChatGPT image generation. Each prompt is the **Style and layout** block below, followed by one sheet's item list.

Ask for a **landscape (1536 × 1024) image with a transparent background**. If ChatGPT can't make the background transparent, plain pure white works too, because the app can remove it.

Save each sheet as a PNG with the file name shown, and put them all in `artwork/storybook/`. Then tell Claude, who will cut them into separate drawings and add them to the library.

If a sheet comes out with text, labels, a background, or items that touch each other, regenerate it with: "Same sheet, but [fix]."

---

## Style and layout (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Create a sprite sheet of exactly 12 separate illustrations in a grid of 4 columns × 3 rows, landscape 1536×1024, on a fully transparent background.
>
> STYLE: richly illustrated children's storybook Bible art. Hand-painted gouache and soft watercolor texture, gentle shading and warm highlights, light from the upper left. Warm, harmonious palette: ochre, terracotta, olive green, sand, sky blue, deep teal, plum, and touches of gold. Every shape has a confident, clean, dark brown-black ink outline of even weight. The outlines must be clearly visible, because the art is traced by a hand-drawing animation. Friendly, expressive faces with simple features and warm skin tones of Middle Eastern appearance. Historically grounded ancient Near East clothing and settings: tunics, robes, sashes, head coverings and sandals, with nothing modern. The tone is reverent, joyful and appealing to children and adults.
>
> LAYOUT RULES: Center each illustration in its own cell, filling about 80% of the cell. No illustration touches or overlaps another cell. Each one is a complete standalone figure or object, with at most a small patch of ground beneath it. Keep the scale, line weight and lighting consistent across all 12. Use no background color, paper texture, scenery behind the items, frames, grid lines, borders, shadows that connect items, text, letters, labels, numbers or watermark.
>
> The 12 illustrations, left to right and top to bottom:
